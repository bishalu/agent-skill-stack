## Structural deviations

- **Trail of Bits plugins are forked, not installed from upstream.** Forked into a local marketplace rather than installed from trailofbits/skills, because several of these plugins carry plugin-level agents, hooks, commands, and CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT-relative workflows that only survive a plugin install — and their routing metadata cannot be changed from outside the plugin. The fork is regenerated from upstream/ by scripts/build.sh; Claude's plugin cache is never edited.

- **`evals/` and `tests/` are dropped from the forked Trail of Bits plugins.** They are upstream CI fixtures, not runtime assets. Nothing in the skills references them.

- **Compound Engineering is installed unmodified.** It is the default lifecycle owner, so its broad triggers are the intended ones, and its skills are already mutually disambiguated upstream.
