## Engineering routing

- One lifecycle owner per phase. Never run two workflows over the same code.
- Compound Engineering (`ce-*`) is the default owner: planning, implementation, debugging, review, PR/CI, shipping, learning.
- Matt Pocock, Vercel, and Trail of Bits skills augment that owner; they do not replace it.
- For non-trivial engineering work, consult the `engineering-router` skill first. Trivial edits need no orchestration.
